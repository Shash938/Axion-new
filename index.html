Analysis Confidence

—

Weighted availability of required financial statement series (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and Valuation multiples). Does not represent price target certainty or investment success probability.

How Confidence is calculated
Evaluates 13 core financial statement series (weighted 80%) including Net Profit, Total Debt, Operating Cash Flow, and CapEx, plus 4 market valuation series (weighted 20%). A 100% score indicates all required line items were retrieved from published filings.
Scoring Rule Scope

Evaluated Coverage

— (0/0 rules)

The percentage of applicable sector scoring rules evaluated with reliable data. Excluded sector metrics (e.g. banking ROCE) are intentionally omitted and do not penalize coverage.

How Coverage is calculated
Calculated as evaluated applicable rules / total applicable rules. For non-financial firms, 14 core rules are evaluated. For banking institutions, non-applicable balance sheet rules are excluded from the denominator.

Axion Fundamental Analysis Methodology

Multi-Dimensional Quality

Evaluates franchise strength across 6 independent pillars: Financial Quality, Historical Consistency, Risk Profile, Earnings Quality, Capital Allocation, and Moat Durability.

Valuation Discipline

Valuation multiples (P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, PEG) are scored separately. An exceptional business is not penalized on business quality if its valuation is demanding.

Sector Adaptation

Financial institutions are evaluated using deposit-funding and leverage metrics rather than industrial working capital and ROCE ratios.

5-Tier Calibrated Benchmarks

Metrics are judged against calibrated fundamental benchmarks (Excellent, Good, Fair, Watch, Alert) rather than arbitrary grading curves.
